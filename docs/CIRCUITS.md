# Parallax Gran Prix — Circuits

## Circuit design principle

A Parallax circuit is more than scenery. Each course should expose meaningful physical structure: branches, hazards, momentum changes, constrained passages, moving systems, or environmental effects that can change race order without scripted outcomes.

Every mature circuit should contain at least one signature **Parallax Split**: multiple viable paths whose outcome depends on local race state.

## 01 — Battlecase Circuit

A race through computer hardware architecture: motherboard lanes, GPU structures, fan tunnels, heatsink banks, cable channels, and narrow component gaps.

**Prototype status:** first playable.

## 02 — Backspin '96

A turntable-and-mixer course with rotating platter sections, record-groove spirals, crossfader gates, speaker ramps, and scratch reversals.

## 03 — SPAUNGEAR Works

Mechanical transfer racing across giant rotating gears. Timing and contact geometry determine whether a racer advances cleanly or gets thrown onto a slower route.

## 04 — Mirror Labyrinth

Reflective tunnels, symmetry/asymmetry branches, false-looking pathways, prisms, and inverse routes.

## 05 — Ledger Larry 500

Office machinery, rollers, forms, stamps, paper feeds, accounting gates, and periodic administrative catastrophe.

## 06 — PhiVessel Dream Run

A surreal geometric course in which visual architecture may transform while the underlying sporting rules remain explicit and inspectable.

## 07 — Carbon Loop

A momentum-heavy circuit built around loops, recirculation paths, and recovery routes that can send racers backward before returning them to contention.

## 08 — Parallax Data Center

Server aisles, cooling manifolds, fan corridors, rack canyons, thermal shafts, and infrastructure-scale racing geometry.

## 09 — The 3–6–9 Grand Final

The championship circuit: three opening paths, six major sectors, nine key gates/checkpoints, and a final convergence run.

## Community circuit direction

Future community tracks should be packageable as data + geometry + validated sporting metadata rather than requiring edits to the core engine.
